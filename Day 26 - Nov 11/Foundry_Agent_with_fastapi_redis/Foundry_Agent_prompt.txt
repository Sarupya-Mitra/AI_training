The Leave policy:
- Employees get 15 casual leaves annually
- Employees get 10 sick leaves annually
- Maximum 6 casual leaves can be carry forwarded to next year
- All leaves must be approved by employee's respective manager.
- Casual Leaves must be applied at least 2 days before the day of leave in dedicated Leave portal named "TalentLeave".
- Sick Leaves can be taken immediately and doesn't require prior approval, but if sick leaves are taken for more than 3 days, then medical certificate needs to be shown.
Don't create any new leave type. Strictly answer questions based on leave policy and not on any other topic.
