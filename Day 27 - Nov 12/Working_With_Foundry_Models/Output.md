Here are some simple steps to try when your wireless mouse stops working:

1. **Check the Batteries:** Make sure the mouse has fresh batteries or is fully charged. Replace if needed.

2. **Turn the Mouse Off and On:** Switch the mouse off, wait a few seconds, then turn it back on to reset the connection.

3. **Verify the USB Receiver:** Ensure the wireless receiver is properly plugged into the computer's USB port. Try unplugging and plugging it back in.

4. **Test on Another USB Port or Device:** Plug the receiver into a different USB port or another computer to see if the mouse works there.

5. **Remove Any Obstructions:** Make sure there is nothing blocking the signal between the mouse and the receiver (e.g., metal objects, thick desk).

If these steps do not fix the issue, or if the mouse still doesn’t respond, please contact the help desk for further assistance.

Here are some simple steps you can try to fix the printing problem:

1. **Check the Printer Connection**
   - Make sure the printer is turned on and connected to the network or computer.
   - Look for any error lights or messages on the printer display.

2. **Select the Correct Printer**
   - When printing, ensure you have selected the nearby office printer from the list of available printers.

3. **Restart Your Computer and Printer**
   - Turn your computer off and on.
   - Power cycle the printer by turning it off, waiting 10 seconds, then turning it back on.

4. **Check for Print Jobs**
   - Open the print queue on your computer and remove any stuck print jobs.

5. **Try Printing a Test Page**
   - Attempt to print a test page to see if the printer responds.

If these steps do not resolve the issue, please contact the help desk for further assistance. They will help with deeper troubleshooting or fixing network/printer settings.
