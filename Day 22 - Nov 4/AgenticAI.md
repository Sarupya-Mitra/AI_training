# Agentic AI
Agentic AI is an artificial intelligence that can independently plan, reason and autonomously take decisions and actions with low or minimal human intervention.

## How Agentic AI work?

Agentic AI tools taks the following steps to perform their operations:
- Perception - Agentic AI collects data from its environment through sensors, APIs, databases or user interactions.
- Reasoning - It reasons through an "LLM" brain or other machine learning based resoning models to determine the best course of action.
- Execution - After the action is finalized, the agent will execute it, by interacting with external systems like APIs,data,robots or providing responses to users.
- Learning and Adaptation - After executing an action, the AI evaluates the outcome, gathers feedback to imporve future decisions.


## Difference with traditional AI and Generative AI
Traditional AI is used to analyze data, recognize patterns and make classifications or predictions. Hence it is deterministic and needs structured input.

Generative AI is used to create new content like text, images in response to a human prompt and hence it is reactive.

Agentic AI is built to pursue high-level goals autonomously. It plans workflows, retains memory and uses external tools and APIs and adapts based on real-time feedback without the need of constant human prompting.

## Single Agent System:
A single agent system is one LLM-driven agent that perceives an input, reasons over it and acts on its own. So one process owns the whole task from start to finish.

## Multi- Agent System:
As the name suggests the multi-agent AI system has multiple agents each with a  defined role, each communicationg with each other to solve a problem that no single one of them could finish alone.
